# Frozen glossary amendment 003 — abbreviations

This amendment is part of the frozen publication style for `manuscript/Manuscript.tex`.

## Rule

The full names **Neyman--Pearson** and **Kullback--Leibler** are introduced once in the Introduction, together with the abbreviations **NP** and **KL**.

After that first introduction, use only:

- **NP** for Neyman--Pearson;
- **KL** for Kullback--Leibler.

Do not re-expand either abbreviation in later sections, theorem statements, proofs, appendices, figure captions, or the abstract if the abstract itself already defines the abbreviation according to its standalone requirements.

Examples in the body of the paper:

- `the NP lemma`, not `the Neyman--Pearson lemma`;
- `KL divergence`, not `Kullback--Leibler divergence`;
- `minimum KL divergence from $\mathcal Q$ to $\mathcal P$`, not `minimum Kullback--Leibler divergence ...`.

This is a house-style rule for this manuscript and should be applied consistently during the publication rewrite.
