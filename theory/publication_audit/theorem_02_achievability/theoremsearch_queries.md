### Markov inequality

- **Exact query:** Q1, `exponential Markov inequality nonnegative random variable tail probability`.
- **Theorem ID/name:** 23838516, Lemma 6 (Markov inequality).
- **Returned statement:** “Let $X$ be a nonnegative random variable and let $\mu=\mathbb E[X]$. Then for all $x>0$, $\mathbb P(X\ge x)\le\mu/x$.”
- **Paper:** *Random GF(q)-representable matroids are not (b,c)-decomposable*.
- **Authors/year:** Jorn van der Pol; 2022.
- **Similarity:** 0.698078.
- **Exact relevance:** applying this statement to the nonnegative variables $e^{\lambda S_n}$ and $e^{(\lambda-1)S_n}$ gives the two direct tail steps once their expectations are bounded.
- **Constants/assumptions match?** The Markov constant is exact. The returned statement does not discuss extended-real $S_n$, uniform classes, or the mixed-infinity convention; those details must remain in the manuscript proof. It therefore clarifies but does not replace the complete direct argument.

### Upper exponential tail

- **Exact query:** Q3, `upper-tail bound from moment generating function Markov inequality`.
- **Theorem ID/name:** 24310684, Lemma 2.3 (Chernoff's inequality).
- **Returned statement:** “For a r.v. $X$ with $\mathrm E e^{tX}<\infty$, $P(X\ge a)\le\inf_{t>0}\{e^{-ta}\mathrm E e^{tX}\}$.”
- **Paper:** *Concentration Inequalities for Statistical Inference*.
- **Authors/year:** Huiming Zhang and Song Xi Chen; 2025.
- **Similarity:** 0.708013.
- **Exact relevance:** it contains the null-side exponential Markov estimate after fixing $t=\lambda$.
- **Constants/assumptions match?** The constant matches the null tail, but the statement assumes an ordinary random variable, optimises only positive $t$, and does not provide the alternative tail, extended-value treatment, or uniform composite conclusion. **INSUFFICIENT FROM THEOREM ARCHIVE** as Route B.

### Tonelli's theorem

- **Exact query:** Q23, `nonnegative measurable functions product integral under product probability measures Tonelli`.
- **Theorem ID/name:** 26876573, Theorem A37 (Tonelli's theorem).
- **Returned statement:** for $\sigma$-finite measure spaces and a nonnegative measurable $f$ on their product, the section integrals are measurable and the product-space integral equals either iterated integral.
- **Paper:** *Measure Theoretic Weighted Model Integration*.
- **Authors/year:** Ivan Miosic and Pedro Zuidberg Dos Martires; 2021.
- **Similarity:** 0.763934.
- **Exact relevance:** probability measures are $\sigma$-finite, and iteration gives factorisation of the nonnegative exponential integrand without an integrability premise. This is exactly why Tonelli, rather than an unqualified appeal to product structure, is measure-theoretically appropriate.
- **Constants/assumptions match?** Yes for each iterative integration step. The theorem is a standard ingredient, not a replacement for either tail argument.

- **Theorem ID/name:** 20527770, Theorem 2.
- **Returned statement:** independent $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ are characterised by $\mathbb E[\prod_i h_i(X_i)]=\prod_i\mathbb E[h_i(X_i)]$ for all nonnegative measurable real-valued $h_i$.
- **Paper:** *Mathematical Foundations of Probability Theory*.
- **Authors/year:** Gane Samb Lo; 2018.

Thus it does not by itself protect the extended-valued case; Tonelli ID 26876573 supplies the safer justification. Classification for replacing Tonelli: **INSUFFICIENT FROM THEOREM ARCHIVE**.
