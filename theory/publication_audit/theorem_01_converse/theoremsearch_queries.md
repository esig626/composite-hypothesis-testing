## Retained theorem statements and metadata

“Retained” means relevant enough to audit a proof route, not that the result replaces the manuscript's step.

### Event change of measure

- **Exact queries:** Q2 and Q23.
- **Theorem ID/name:** 26995949, Lemma 1.
- **Statement returned:** “(Change of measure with Rényi Divergence). For any $\alpha>1$ and any event $E\in\mathcal F$, $P(E)\le Q(E)^{(\alpha-1)/\alpha}e^{((\alpha-1)/\alpha)D_\alpha(P\|Q)}$.”
- **Paper metadata:** *A DPI-PAC-Bayesian Framework for Generalization Bounds*; Muhan Guan, Farhad Farokhi, and Jingge Zhu; 2025.
- **Scores:** 0.783715 (Q2), 0.835706 (Q23).
- **Relevance:** after renaming the archive's $P$ as the manuscript's $Q$ and vice versa, this has the exact order, orientation, and exponent for an indicator of an event. It does **not** state the bounded-randomised-test expectation inequality. Consequently it is **INSUFFICIENT FROM THEOREM ARCHIVE** as a replacement for the Hölder step for arbitrary $0\le\varphi\le1$.

### Second event change-of-measure statement

- **Exact queries:** Q2 and Q23.
- **Theorem ID/name:** 24257764, Lemma A.1.
- **Statement returned:** “For any probability measures $P,Q$, any $\alpha>1$, and any measurable set $A$, $P(A)\le e^{(1-1/\alpha)D_\alpha(P\|Q)}Q(A)^{1-1/\alpha}$.”
- **Paper metadata:** *Robust estimation of a Markov chain transition matrix from multiple sample paths*; Lasse Leskelä and Maximilien Dreveton; 2025.
- **Scores:** 0.740784 (Q2), 0.818690 (Q23).
- **Relevance:** this independently matches the preceding event inequality, including constants and orientation after variable renaming, but again covers events rather than randomised tests. Classification: **INSUFFICIENT FROM THEOREM ARCHIVE** for replacing the Hölder step.

### Rényi data processing under a Markov kernel

- **Exact queries:** Q8, Q11, and Q22.
- **Theorem ID/name:** 24148956, Lemma 8 (Data-processing inequality).
- **Statement returned:** “Let $\mu,\nu$ be probability measures, $P$ a Markov kernel, and $q\ge1$. Then $\mathcal R_q(\mu P\|\nu P)\le\mathcal R_q(\mu\|\nu)$.”
- **Paper metadata:** *Rényi-infinity constrained sampling with $d^3$ membership queries*; Yunbum Kook and Matthew S. Zhang; 2024.
- **Scores:** 0.699277 (Q8), 0.654231 (Q11), 0.699972 (Q22).
- **Relevance:** this verifies contraction with the needed orientation when the kernel is applied in the same order. The returned theorem does not identify a randomised test with the required binary kernel and does not include the binary Rényi calculation that would recover the desired rejection-probability bound. The complete route is therefore **INSUFFICIENT FROM THEOREM ARCHIVE**.

### Product additivity

- **Exact queries:** Q17 and Q18.
- **Theorem ID/name:** 22017189, Theorem 28 (Additivity).
- **Statement returned:** for pairs $(P_i,Q_i)$ and every $\alpha\in[0,\infty]$, $\sum_{i=1}^N D_\alpha(P_i\|Q_i)=D_\alpha(P_1\times\cdots\times P_N\|Q_1\times\cdots\times Q_N)$; it also states the corresponding countable-product identity except at $\alpha=0$.
- **Paper metadata:** *Rényi Divergence and Kullback-Leibler Divergence*; Tim van Erven and Peter Harremoës; 2014.
- **Scores:** 0.712831 (Q17), 0.784309 (Q18).
- **Relevance:** this exactly verifies $D_\lambda(Q^{\otimes n}\|P^{\otimes n})=nD_\lambda(Q\|P)$, including orientation, for the finite product used in the proof. It replaces the proof's unexpanded appeal to tensorisation, but does not replace Hölder.

## Search conclusion

No archived theorem returned by these 24 searches states the exact pairwise inequality for an arbitrary randomised test, with all of the manuscript's constants, order range, Rényi orientation, and definitions. No archived one-shot theorem states the target Type II bound with the required product factor. Thus no exact archive theorem replaces Hölder.
